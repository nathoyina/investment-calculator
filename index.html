Sources, fees, and what this mix leaves out

A 100% equity plan can fall hard. MSCI ACWI’s deepest drop on the 30 Sep 2026 factsheet was 58.06%, from October 2007 to March 2009. The calmer quiz answers use AGGU, the iShares Core Global Aggregate Bond fund (Ireland, accumulating, hedged to US dollars, yearly fee 0.10%). On the 30 Sep 2026 BlackRock factsheet, already after the fee, it returned 4.16% a year over 3 years, 0.10% a year over 5 years, and 1.52% a year since launch. The 5-year figure includes 2022, when the fund fell 11.65%. The calm-plan illustration uses the since-launch figure. Its best calendar year there is 2019 at 8.03%. It launched in November 2017, so it has no 10-year record. Its deepest week-end fall in US dollars was 15.45%, from the 7 Dec 2020 close to the 16 Oct 2022 close. The Nasdaq-100, via QQQ’s price, fell 83% from March 2000 to October 2002. Max gain uses the best sourced calendar year: 2019 for the MSCI indexes in the factsheet’s 2012–2025 table, and 1999 for the Nasdaq-100 price index (+101.95%, Slickcharts, price return). The dollar figures apply those percents to the projected ending value. They are not a forecast, and a monthly plan would have been buying during the fall.

When the main fund is worldwide, do not add an S&P 500 fund on top. That core already holds the US market, and the Nasdaq sleeve is the extra tech weight. On 30 Sep 2026, MSCI World was 72.9% United States and 31.8% information technology. Choosing the United States makes the main fund the S&P 500 itself: XUS on the Singapore exchange, or XDPU in London. Both are the Xtrackers S&P 500 fund, Ireland, accumulating, yearly fee 0.03%. The planning rate is the S&P 500 index total return on the 30 Sep 2026 SPDR S&P 500 factsheet: 22.89% a year over 3 years, 13.79% over 5 years, and 15.33% over 10 years. That factsheet has no since-1988 figure, so the since-1988 choice reuses 15.33%. The price index fell 56.78% from 9 Oct 2007 to 9 Mar 2009. The 2019 total return was 31.49%. US-based VOO, QQQ, and the Singapore listing S27 withhold 30% of dividends from a Singapore investor and can fall inside US estate tax above 60,000 US dollars. The Straits Times Index (GAB or G3B) is a bank-heavy Singapore sleeve, not this core.

Broker costs on the result card are published buy fees as of 23 Sep 2026 and 2 Oct 2026 (Beansprout). They are not compounded into the projection, because a minimum charge does not grow with the balance. Currency conversion is extra. This is research, not a personal recommendation or tax advice.
